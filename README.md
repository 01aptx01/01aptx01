### Executive Summary

Applied Computer Science senior at **King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT)** specializing in **backend systems architecture**, **containerized distributed workflows**, and **applied machine learning**. Experienced in architecting decoupled client-server platforms, event-driven background queues, and production-oriented NLP pipelines.

* **Distributed Systems & Architecture**: Engineering decoupled agent-server topologies, asynchronous event queues (Docker, Redis), and real-time bidirectional telemetry (WebSockets).
* **Machine Learning & Applied AI**: Designing end-to-end pipelines ranging from anomaly classification (NIDS) to fine-tuned Transformer architectures (XLM-RoBERTa).
* **Engineering Standards & Reliability**: Implementing automated integration test suites (Selenium, Robot Framework) and standardized CI/CD workflows for reproducible delivery.
* **Objective**: Targeting **Software Engineer** and **Backend Developer** positions focused on resilient architecture, distributed infrastructure, and high-performance services.
